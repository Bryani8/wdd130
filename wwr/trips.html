Choose Your Adventure

We have several guided river trips different experience level. Explore our packages below and book your next trip!

Guayllabamba River

Perfect for families and beginners. Enjoy a peaceful day floating down beautiful canyons, watching wildlife, and stopping for a fresh riverside picnic lunch.

Mindo River

Get ready to get wet! This popular trip balances rolling waves and exciting rapids with calm, scenic pools. Ideal for first-timers looking for a thrill or experienced rafters.

Quijos River

Designed for true rafters. Navigate tight, steep drops, powerful hydraulics, and massive waves alongside our most seasoned expert guides.
