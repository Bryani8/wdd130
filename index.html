Hey! I am Bryan Morocho from Quito, Ecuador. I like to work out and play computer games to unwind. My favorite Ecuadorian food is llapingacho. It is made with cheesy potato cakes, a fried egg, a beet salad and fried meat. If you ever come to Ecuador, you have to try it.

Ecuador

Ecuador is a country crossed by the equatorial line. It is bordered by Peru to the east and south, Colombia to the north, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. Within its borders lies the Chimborazo volcano, which is the highest place on Earth measured from the planet's center.
